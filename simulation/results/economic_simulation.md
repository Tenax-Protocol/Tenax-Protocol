## Revenue target T_ETH

T_ETH is set to the median forecaster ETH per season in months 10 to 12 of the medium scenario: **0.068 ETH per season** at a 100 ETH launch FDV ($184), or 0.0681% of the launch FDV. Below it, the reserve tops up season rewards; at or above it, the whole allowance is burned.
